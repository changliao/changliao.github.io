**HexWatershed** is a mesh-independent flow direction and watershed delineation model designed for modern hydrologic and Earth system modeling. It resolves long-standing challenges in hydrologic flow routing by operating across diverse mesh structures, including variable-resolution Centroidal Voronoi Tessellations (MPAS) and hexagonal grids.

### Highlights & Applications
* Resolves river network topological connectivity on unstructured Voronoi meshes and hexagonal meshes.
* Integrated within the DOE Energy Exascale Earth System Model ([E3SM](https://e3sm.org)) land (ELM) and river (MOSART) components.
* Enables seamless multi-scale flow routing from watershed scales to global domains.
